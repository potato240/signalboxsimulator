Signal Box Simulator

Pick a signal box. Keep the trains moving without making the road wait too long.

  1. The bell rings when a train is offered. Accept it at its block instrument.
  2. Close the road: turn the gate wheel (Bailey) or barrier wheel (Straw Lake), trolley gate wheel (Valley Bridge) or barrier wheel (Parkside Plaza), lower the barriers (Tuborne) or switch on the crossing lights (Lee Forest, Fourth Lane Quay Footpath and Brickworks) or start the wig-wags (High Barkley, Barkley Station and Wartham Highway) or swing the gates by hand (Otla). A stalled vehicle will block this.
  3. Road shut? Pull that train's signal lever. After the train passes, restore the lever and reopen the road.

Shortcuts: keys 1–4 pull levers; → shuts the road and ← opens it at every crossing (wheel, barriers, gates and lights). In 3D, key V cycles Window, Walk (WASD, mouse to look, click levers, instruments and controls) and Overview.
