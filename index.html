Bailey Crossing Signal Box

You are the signalman on a single-track line crossing a country road. Keep the trains moving without making the road wait too long.

  1. The bell rings when a signalman offers a train. Press Accept train.
  2. Check the road, then close the gates with the wheel. A stalled car will block them.
  3. Gates fully closed? Pull that train's signal lever to clear it.
  4. After the train has passed, restore the lever, then open the gates for the road.

Drag to look out of the window, press V to switch to an overview. Wheel: hold ←/→. Levers: keys 1 and 2.

Interlocking: signals can't clear until the gates are closed. Gates can't open while a signal is off or a train is committed to the crossing.
